FAQ

Questions, adventurer?

What is ISEKAI ZERO?+

An interactive AI storytelling app. You chat with characters, play branching stories and build worlds of your own.

Is it free?+

Yes. ISEKAI ZERO is free on iOS, Android and the web. Text generation uses Mana, which you can earn for free or buy.

How is it different from other AI chat apps?+

Characters keep their memory across sessions, worldbuilding goes beyond single characters, and creators share in the profit.

How do creators earn money?+

Creators receive 25% of the Mana spent on text generation in their characters and storylines, paid to their payout account.

Which languages are supported?+

Seventeen languages, including several Chinese, Portuguese and Spanish variants.

Who makes ISEKAI ZERO?+

ARX Media Sdn Bhd, in Kuching, Sarawak, Malaysia.
