Overview

The XfiniteOS AI chatbot is our own program that uses AI to simulate human-like conversation. It understands and responds to written or spoken language, so users can talk to it naturally.

Teams build and publish chatbot personalities from one dashboard, then tune their knowledge, gallery and skills before going live.
