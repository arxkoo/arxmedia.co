JMD@Kuching: Memperkasa Rakyat Melalui Kreatif Digital

ARx Media showcased Kristy AI at Jelajah Malaysia Digital (JMD)@Kuching, a programme to build digital skills in creative content such as animation, games and creative technology.

Jelajah Malaysia Digital (JMD)@Kuching set out to empower people through digital skills, with a focus on creative digital content such as animation, digital games and creative technology. High-performing local companies were invited to show their innovations.

MDEC chairman Syed Ibrahim Syed Noh described the programme as more than a technology exhibition: a platform to discover talent, build networks and strengthen collaboration between government, industry and community, particularly in developing the Digital Creative Cluster.

ARx Media opened a booth at the event, where manager Jimsley Lim introduced Kristy AI, Malaysia’s first multilingual AI agent, powered by the ARx AI framework. Kristy is built to break down language barriers so communication works smoothly across cultures and communities.

“Kristy fasih dalam semua bahasa utama dan memastikan komunikasi yang benar-benar global.”

Jimsley Lim, Manager, ARx Media
