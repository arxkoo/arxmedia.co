Seven years together, ARx sets sail, gratitude along the way

Seven years ago, ARx Media Sdn Bhd was born from a small dream in our hearts. Back then, we were just a team of five, driven by a passion for future technology, embarking on a journey full of challenges. Today, our team has grown to 23 members, and every step forward has been built on the dedication and hard work of our entire team.

Throughout this journey, we’ve overcome countless challenges and setbacks. To those who have supported and trusted us along the way, we owe our deepest gratitude. Your unwavering belief in us has kept us grounded and determined to push forward. With a team primarily made up of Gen Z talents, we’ve proven that youth is not a limitation but a source of hope. We are committed to nurturing these talents and cultivating future leaders for society.

Looking ahead, we will continue to focus on artificial intelligence and technological innovation. During the pandemic, we laid a strong foundation for AI development, and in the next five years, we aim to scale our AI technologies. We believe technology can transform lives, and we aspire to bring glory to Sarawak, showcasing its brilliance to the world.

Seven years is just the beginning. To everyone who has supported us, thank you for helping ARx reach this far. Together, let us create an even brighter future!

七年同行，汸舟启航，感恩相伴

七年前，汸舟未来科技有限公司（ARX）诞生于我们心中的一个小小梦想。当时，我们只是一个由五人组成的小团队，怀抱着对未来科技的憧憬，踏上了充满挑战的创业之路。今天，我们的团队已发展到二十三人，每一步的成长都离不开每一位成员的努力与心血。

一路走来，我们克服了无数困难与挫折，也感谢那些始终支持、信任我们的朋友和伙伴。正是因为有你们的陪伴，我们才能在最艰难的时候坚守初心、不断前行。我们以零零后为主的团队，用实际行动证明，年轻不是限制，而是希望。我们珍惜人才，也致力于为社会培育更多未来的栋梁。

未来，我们将继续专注人工智能与科技创新。疫情期间，我们打下了AI发展的基础，并在接下来的五年中稳步推进AI技术的规模化发展。我们相信科技可以改变生活，也希望为砂拉越增光，让这片土地的光芒被世界看见。

七年是起点，未来在前方。感谢你们的陪伴，让汸舟未来科技有限公司（ARX）能够走得更远。让我们携手共创更美好的明天！
